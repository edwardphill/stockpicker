Rating: Strong Buy | 12-Month Price Target: $40.29 | Current Price: $28.62 | Implied Upside: ~41%

We are initiating coverage of Immunovant, Inc. (IMVT) with a Strong Buy rating, predicated on our view that the market is insufficiently discounting the commercial potential of the company's anti-FcRn pipeline, particularly batoclimab, across multiple autoimmune indications with large addressable markets.

Investment Thesis

Immunovant is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing batoclimab, a fully human monoclonal antibody targeting the neonatal Fc receptor (FcRn), a validated mechanism for reducing pathogenic IgG antibodies. The anti-FcRn class has been commercially de-risked by argenx's VYVGART (efgartigimod), which achieved blockbuster revenues across myasthenia gravis and CIDP. We believe the market is underappreciating the breadth and velocity of IMVT's clinical program — spanning Graves' disease, myasthenia gravis, CIDP, warm autoimmune hemolytic anemia, and other IgG-mediated conditions — as well as batoclimab's differentiated subcutaneous fixed-dose profile, which may confer meaningful convenience advantages relative to existing therapies.

At a $5.83B market capitalization, the stock trades at a substantial discount to the risk-adjusted net present value we assign to its late-stage pipeline. Short interest stands at an elevated 20.9% of float, which we interpret as reflecting residual skepticism around commercial execution and competitive positioning against argenx rather than fundamental clinical risk. Notably, two insider open-market purchases were recorded in the past 30 days — a signal we weight meaningfully for pre-commercial biotech companies where insiders have asymmetric visibility into trial readouts and regulatory interactions. IMVT has also outperformed the S&P 500 by approximately 900 basis points over the trailing six months, suggesting gradual institutional re-rating is underway but far from complete.

The competitive landscape is evolving but remains favorable. While argenx is the clear first-mover, peak anti-FcRn market estimates across all indications range from $15–25B globally by the early 2030s, supporting multiple commercially viable entrants. Batoclimab's subcutaneous, fixed-dose regimen and differentiated clinical data position it well to capture meaningful share, particularly in indications where argenx has yet to establish dominance.

We view this discount as unjustified given the FcRn mechanism's clinical and commercial validation.

▼ Bear Case

Bear Case Thesis

The market is not "insufficiently discounting" batoclimab's potential — it is correctly pricing a pre-revenue, single-asset clinical-stage company against the base rate of Phase 3 failure, the competitive reality that argenx has a multi-year head start with an entrenched commercial infrastructure, and the near-certainty of further dilution before any revenue materializes. The bull's $40.29 target rests on probability-weighted peak revenues several years out, yet underweights the capital structure erosion required to get there. At $5.8B market cap with -$490M annual EBITDA and no revenue, IMVT is priced as if meaningful probability-weighted commercial value is already assured. The 20.9% short interest is not residual skepticism — it reflects a rational assessment that the risk/reward is balanced, not skewed to the upside.

Challenging the Bull's Assumptions

1. "FcRn mechanism is de-risked by VYVGART."
Mechanism validation does not equal molecule validation. Batoclimab has a different binding epitope, pharmacokinetic profile, and dosing regimen than efgartigimod. The historical clinical hold on batoclimab (2021, due to LDL cholesterol elevations) is not a footnote — it is evidence of a differentiated and potentially less favorable safety profile. The bull assigns 65–70% probability of pivotal success; the oncology/immunology Phase 3 base rate is closer to 50–60%, and the prior safety signal arguably warrants a discount to class averages, not a premium. A single unexpected safety finding in a pivotal readout would be devastating given the lack of pipeline diversification.

2. "Batoclimab's SC fixed-dose profile is differentiated."
argenx has already filed and is commercializing a subcutaneous formulation of efgartigimod (VYVGART Hytrulo). The convenience differentiation is narrowing. Moreover, argenx's next-generation asset (ARGX-119, a complement-independent approach) and emerging oral FcRn degraders from companies like Alumis and Structure Therapeutics could leapfrog the SC injectable form factor entirely. The window for "differentiated convenience" may be shorter than the bull assumes.

3. "IMVT trades at a discount to EV/pipeline comps."
The comp set is misleading. Pre-approval argenx was a pioneer establishing a novel class; IMVT is a follower entering behind an entrenched blockbuster. Followers in validated mechanisms historically trade at 40–60% discounts to pioneers on EV/indication metrics — which is roughly where IMVT sits now. The "discount" is the correct valuation, not an opportunity. Furthermore, applying 2029E revenue multiples at 3.5x assumes those revenues materialize; on a probability-adjusted basis (accounting for trial failure, competitive share loss, and launch execution risk), the effective multiple is substantially higher.

4. "Insider purchases signal asymmetric upside."
Two open-market purchases in 30 days at a pre-commercial biotech controlled by Roivant Sciences is a thin signal. Roivant insiders have structural incentives to support the stock price ahead of potential equity raises. Without disclosure of purchase size relative to holdings, this is noise, not signal. We note the absence of notable institutional activity in the last 90 days — a more informative data point the bull acknowledged but did not weight.

5. "Short interest creates squeeze potential."
Elevated short interest at a pre-revenue biotech burning ~$490M/year is not anomalous — it reflects fundamental short sellers who understand the binary risk profile. The squeeze narrative is a retail framing. In practice, the borrow is readily available and days-to-cover is manageable. A squeeze requires a genuine positive catalyst, not its anticipation.

6. "Cash runway sufficiency through key readouts."
The bull flags this as "a key item to monitor" but does not model the dilution scenario. At -$490M annual burn and multiple Phase 3 programs running, IMVT will almost certainly require additional capital before any approval or revenue. A secondary offering at or below current levels — which is the base case for a company burning at this rate — would compress per-share value by 10–20% depending on timing and size. The Roivant relationship provides some flexibility, but not without cost to minority shareholders.

Downside Scenario & Valuation

Scenario A — Pivotal failure in lead indication (30–35% probability): A miss on primary endpoint in Graves' disease or MG would reduce IMVT to option value on remaining pipeline. Comparable clinical-stage biotechs post-pivotal failure trade at 0.5–1.0x cash plus residual pipeline option value. Assuming ~$800M–$1B in cash at readout and modest pipeline optionality, this implies an enterprise value of $1.2–1.8B, or $6–9/share. This represents 70–80% downside from current levels.

Scenario B — Positive but non-differentiated data (20–25% probability): Batoclimab meets primary endpoint but shows no meaningful efficacy or safety advantage over VYVGART. In this scenario, market share assumptions compress materially — IMVT captures 15–20% of addressable market vs. the bull's implicit 30%+. Applying a 10x EV/revenue multiple to $500–700M probability-adjusted peak sales (vs. the bull's implied $1.5B+), discounted at 12%, yields a present value of roughly $18–22/share — below current price.

Scenario C — Dilution overhang: Independent of clinical outcomes, a $500M+ equity raise at current or lower prices would add ~17M shares (at $28), diluting existing holders by ~8–10%. This is the most probable near-term headwind and is not priced into the bull's target.

Weighted bear-case fair value: $14–18/share, representing 35–50% downside, probability-weighting across these scenarios.

Verdict

Monitor. The investment thesis is binary and will be resolved by pivotal data readouts in 2H 2026–1H 2027; the risk/reward at $28.62 does not compensate adequately for the ~30–35% probability of catastrophic downside, the near-certainty of dilution, and the competitive compression from argenx's expanding label — position sizing should await clarity on at least one pivotal readout, with the May 20 business update as the first checkpoint for cash runway and enrollment trajectory.
