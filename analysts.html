How it works

Lone Bull: consensus is Hold or Sell, but only 1–2 analysts (and at most 20%) say Buy. Lone Bear: the reverse. Breakaway: a firm's rating or price target in the last 120 days sits against consensus or 25%+ away from the mean target. 50% path: at least one target implies 50%+ upside from today's price. New: never picked, or not picked in the last 180 days. Score rewards lonelier, more recent, further-out calls. Universe: every tracked pick plus data/watchlist.json. Ratings come from Yahoo Finance and can lag a day or two.
